2022Index tracking with mixed-integer programming (Advanced Mathematical Programming, Clemson University)
Formulated and solved mixed-integer linear programs (after Canakgoz and Beasley, 2009) that track the S&P 500 with K stocks under cardinality, proportion and trading-cost limits, driving the portfolio's alpha toward 0 and its beta toward 1; judged each portfolio by regressing its returns on the index's.
AMPL · MATLAB (intlinprog) · regression
2021Robust curve fitting by linear programming (Mathematical Programming, Clemson University)
Posed least-absolute-deviation (l1) polynomial fitting as a linear program and compared it with least squares (l2): the l1 fit stays with the bulk of the data while outliers pull the l2 fit.
MATLAB (linprog) · linear programming · duality
2019–2025Computer algebra for free resolutions (Ph.D. research, Clemson University)
