Research

I study multiplicative structures on free resolutions in commutative and homological algebra. A free resolution describes a quotient ring through generators, relations, and higher relations. The ring's multiplication can be lifted to its minimal free resolution, but associativity need not survive. My work asks when this failure is unavoidable and how much compatible multiplication can still be retained.

My current work distinguishes two kinds of obstruction. A uniform obstruction identifies a fixed triple on which every multiplication fails to be associative. A relative obstruction shows that requiring associativity on one collection of triples forces it to fail on another. I compare unital chain-map multiplications without requiring graded commutativity or compatibility with an internal grading.

I combine homotopy arguments and explicit coefficient calculations with computer algebra. A Lean 4 companion formalizes selected obstruction arguments.

Associativity without grading or commutativity assumptions
For Katthän's example, I prove that no unital chain-map multiplication on the minimal free resolution is associative over any field. This removes both graded commutativity and compatibility with the internal grading from the hypotheses. The argument extends to a family that includes nonmonomial ideals.
Uniform obstructions and elimination
I give criteria for uniform non-associativity by comparing associators or their primitives under changes of multiplication. These obstructions persist from monomial elimination ideals and give explicit applications to Avramov's example and an eight-vertex Bier sphere over every field.
Multiplicative Taylor comparisons
For the edge ideal of the eight-cycle, the Taylor resolution on five suitably selected generators embeds into the minimal free resolution as a chain complex, yet no selection of five generators admits a multiplicative comparison. Four generators admit a multiplicative embedding.
Earlier work
My thesis studied homological associativity of differential graded algebras: associator subcomplexes, the maximal associative quotient, and Gröbner bases to compute them, with a fixed-triple obstruction in Avramov's example (arXiv:2406.00141).
